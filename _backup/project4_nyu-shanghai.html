NYU Shanghai-Visual & Social Media Design

A collection of poster and social media designs created for the Center for Student Belonging, supporting campus events, cultural programs, and student engagement.

Introduction

During my internship at NYU Shanghai Center for Student Belonging, I created visual materials for student events, cultural programs, and community initiatives.

My work ranged from event posters to social media assets, translating different event themes into clear and engaging visual communication.


Deliverables

Poster Design

Social Media Graphic Design

Event Promotion

Campaign Visuals

Design Context

Designing for a diverse campus community

Each event had a different audience, message, and visual tone. The challenge was to create distinctive identities while keeping information clear, accessible, and appropriate for NYU Shanghai campus communication.

From Brief to Visual Language

Event Brief

Event name:"Who Says You Can't? Breaking Limits with Boxing to Redefine Your Boundaries" Aboro Boxing Studio & NYU Shanghai

Featuring world champion boxer Michele Aboro, the event explored boxing as more than a sport, a way to challenge boundaries, redefine identity, and build confidence through physical and mental strength.

Design Brief

Create an energetic event visual that reflects the strength and intensity of boxing while communicating themes of identity, empowerment, and community.

Visual Concept

Inspired by the event message of challenging expectations, I used an expressive and unconventional visual language to represent breaking away from imposed boundaries and defining your own identity.
